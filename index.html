MapIt Pro Help

Search a place, choose a style, adjust camera/framing, then export PNG artwork.

Quick Start

Search a city or landmark, choose a palette, set the camera, then pick a frame or export style.

3D Pro

Enable 3D Pro for Mapbox Standard 3D, then choose light, theme, color mood, and camera presets. Place labels and road names stay controlled by the layer switches.

Frames

Use Poster, Ticket, Postcard, or Stamp for different publishing formats. Stamp automatically uses the current place and season text.

Projects

Save a .mapit file to keep the place, camera, style, layers, markers, captions, frame, and export settings for later editing.

Export

Use 2x or 4x for most work. 6x and 8x Pro create larger poster files and may take longer, especially with 3D Pro or heavy effects.
